🔍

Semantic search

More than keywords — describe what you want in one sentence and it finds films by intent.

🏷️

Auto-tagging

AI picks tags from a 14-dim / 400-tag taxonomy; whitelist-validated, editor-reviewed.

✅

Honest scores

When nothing truly matches, it says so — no more relative scores faking a perfect 100%.

💬

Explainable results

Every film states why it surfaced — which conditions it matched, and how it was found.

🏆

Award tracking

Nomination lists from 18 award sites — auto-scraped, AI-structured, Wikidata-verified, matched to library tags.

📈

Self-eval loop

An LLM-judge harness over 45 real queries drives tuning — winners stay, losers roll back, nDCG@5 0.93 → 0.96.
